# What Is a Pulley?

<LessonSection id="meet-the-parts" title="Meet the Parts">
  <p>
    A pulley is a wheel with a groove that guides a rope. The wheel turns while
    the rope pulls, which helps redirect force and move a load more smoothly.
  </p>
  <p>
    In this course, you will look at where the rope goes, how many rope segments
    support the load, and how those details change the lifting job.
  </p>
</LessonSection>
