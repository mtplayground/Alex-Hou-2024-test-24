# What Is a Pulley?

<LessonSection id="meet-the-machine" title="Meet the Machine">
  <p>
    A pulley is one of the simplest machines for moving a load with a rope. It
    has a wheel with a groove, and the rope sits inside that groove so it can
    travel smoothly as you pull.
  </p>
  <p>
    In this first lesson, the main goal is not a formula. It is a picture in
    your head: when one end of the rope moves, the load on the other side can
    move too.
  </p>
  <p>
    Watch for three parts in every pulley scene: the wheel, the rope, and the
    load. If you can point to those parts, you are ready for the rest of the
    unit.
  </p>
</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="lift-the-bucket" title="Lift the Bucket">
  <p>
    Drag the rope end downward to lift the bucket. Reset the scene and try it a
    few times until you can predict which side of the rope will move up and

<LessonSection id="what-the-wheel-does" title="What the Wheel Does">
  <p>
    The wheel does not make the bucket float. Its job is to guide the rope along
    a curved path, which lets the force you apply travel around the top of the
    pulley.
  </p>
  <p>
    In this example, pulling down is convenient because your hands can use body
    weight while the bucket rises upward. That direction change is one of the
    first big ideas in pulley systems.
  </p>
  <p>
    Later lessons will focus on how several rope segments can reduce the force
    you need. For now, focus on the motion: rope down, bucket up.
  </p>
</LessonSection>
