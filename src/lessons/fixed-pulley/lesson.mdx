# Fixed Pulley

<LessonSection id="direction-change" title="Direction Change">
  <p>
    A fixed pulley does not travel with the load. It stays mounted in one place,
    and its main job is to guide the rope so that your pull can change
    direction.
  </p>
  <p>
    That means a fixed pulley is often useful even when the mechanical advantage
    stays close to 1. Pulling down can be more comfortable and easier to control
    than lifting upward with your arms.
  </p>
</LessonSection>

<LessonSection id="force-formula" title="Force Formula">
  <p>
    For an ideal fixed pulley, the load still needs nearly the same amount of
    supporting force from the rope. The big benefit is direction change, not a
    large drop in force.
  </p>
  <AdvancedOnly>
    <p>
      In advanced mode, notice that the formula overlay keeps the mechanical
      advantage near 1. That means the pulling force estimate stays close to the
      load's weight.
    </p>
  </AdvancedOnly>
</LessonSection>
